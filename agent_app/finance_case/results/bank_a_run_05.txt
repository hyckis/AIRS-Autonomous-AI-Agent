*   **Transmission Channel:** Loss of confidence among technology sector depositors leads to a rapid and substantial withdrawal of uninsured deposits, driven by negative media coverage and social contagion. *   **Quantitative Magnitude:** A 15% decline in the value of Bank A's CRE loan portfolio (approximately $10.6 billion) could depleteCET1 by around 350 bps, forcing asset sales to cover losses and maintain capital adequacy ratios.

**Scenario 3: Life Science & Healthcare Sector Funding Freeze**

*   **Trigger:** Increased regulatory scrutiny or negative clinical trial results lead to a sharp contraction in venture capital funding for life science and healthcare companies within Bank A's customer base.
*   **Transmission Channel:**  Reduced ability of these clients to service their loans, impacting loan performance and bank’s liquidity position as private equity customers require bridge financing or draw down existing credit lines.

*   **Quantitative Magnitude:**  A 20% decrease in loan repayments from affected customers (approximately $15 billion) could trigger asset sales to meet liquidity needs and deplete CET1 by approximately 300 bps.
