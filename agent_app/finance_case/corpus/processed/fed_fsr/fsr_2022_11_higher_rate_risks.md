# Federal Reserve Financial Stability Report — November 2022
## Risks from Persistently High Inflation and Higher Interest Rates

Persistently high inflation could require interest rates to rise beyond
levels expected by market participants.

Higher-than-expected interest rates could weaken the debt-service
capacity of households and businesses and increase delinquencies,
bankruptcies, and other forms of financial distress.

A sharp increase in rates could also increase financial-market
volatility, strain market liquidity, and reduce asset prices, including
commercial and residential real estate prices.

Losses at financial intermediaries could reduce access to capital and
increase funding costs, potentially amplifying the effects on credit
availability and economic activity.
