# Federal Reserve Financial Stability Report — November 2022
## Interest Rates, Asset Valuations, and Commercial Real Estate

Central banks, including the Federal Reserve, tightened monetary policy
in response to persistently high inflation. Treasury yields increased
substantially and financial conditions tightened.

Commercial real estate valuations remained elevated, although price
growth slowed as borrowing costs increased.

Valuation pressures in CRE remained high. Office and retail property
fundamentals were comparatively weaker than those of some other
property types.

Banks reported weaker demand for most CRE loan categories and tighter
lending standards during 2022.
