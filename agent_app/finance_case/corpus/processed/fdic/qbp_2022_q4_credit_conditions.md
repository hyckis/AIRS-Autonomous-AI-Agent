# FDIC Q4 2022 Quarterly Banking Profile
## Credit and Asset-Quality Conditions

Higher market interest rates may erode real estate and other asset values and
weaken borrowers' ability to repay loans.

Loan growth remained strong in 2022.

Community-bank loan growth was driven in part by nonfarm nonresidential
commercial real estate loans.

Noncurrent and net charge-off rates increased modestly but remained below
pre-pandemic averages.

Early delinquencies increased slightly, including in certain residential,
commercial real estate, and consumer loan categories.

Persistent economic uncertainty may contribute to tighter underwriting,
slower loan growth, higher provision expense, and liquidity constraints.
