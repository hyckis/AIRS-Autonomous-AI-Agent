# Federal Reserve Financial Stability Report — November 2022
## Bank Capital and Securities Valuation

Bank risk-based capital ratios remained broadly within the range
observed over the previous decade.

The ratio of tangible common equity to tangible assets declined during
2022. The decline was driven in part by a substantial reduction in
tangible equity from unrealized losses on securities held in
available-for-sale portfolios as interest rates increased.

Securities classified as held to maturity are carried differently from
available-for-sale securities. Changes in the market value of HTM
securities generally do not alter reported capital ratios because the
securities are not marked to market.

Some large banks are required to reflect changes in AFS market values in
regulatory capital, while some smaller institutions are not subject to
the same treatment.
