# Federal Reserve Financial Stability Report — November 2022
## Bank Funding and Uninsured Deposits

Funding risk can arise when institutions fund longer-maturity or
less-liquid assets with liabilities that investors can withdraw on
short notice. Rapid withdrawals may force institutions to sell assets
quickly at depressed prices, potentially generating losses.

As of 2022 Q2, uninsured deposits in the U.S. financial system totaled
approximately $7.9 trillion.

At the aggregate level, funding risks at domestic banks were assessed as
low because banks held substantial liquid assets and relied relatively
little on short-term wholesale funding.

However, institutional deposits may be more sensitive to changes in the
spread between deposit rates and short-term market interest rates.
Banks had experienced some decline in institutional deposits as that
spread widened.
