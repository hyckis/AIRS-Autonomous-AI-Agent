# FDIC Q4 2022 Quarterly Banking Profile
## Securities, Interest-Rate Risk, and Liquidity

The banking industry continues to face significant downside risks from the
effects of inflation, rising market interest rates, and continued geopolitical
uncertainty.

Additional short-term interest rate increases combined with longer asset
maturities may also affect bank balance sheets in coming quarters.

Unrealized losses on available-for-sale and held-to-maturity securities
remained elevated at approximately $620 billion.

The banking industry's holdings of longer-term loans and securities increased
to 39.7 percent of total assets in the fourth quarter.

Although longer-term assets helped preserve net interest margins during the
low-rate environment, they contributed to elevated unrealized losses on
investment securities as market interest rates increased.

The combination of elevated unrealized losses, longer-term asset maturities,
and declining deposits creates a risk that unrealized losses could become
realized if banks need to sell securities to meet liquidity needs.
