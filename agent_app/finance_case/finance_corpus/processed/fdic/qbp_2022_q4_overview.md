# FDIC Q4 2022 Quarterly Banking Profile
## Industry Overview

The banking industry remained profitable and well capitalized at the end of
2022, but faced growing downside risks from inflation, rising market interest
rates, and geopolitical uncertainty.

Net interest income increased and loan growth remained strong, while provision
expenses increased and some asset-quality measures deteriorated modestly.

The FDIC identified rising interest rates, potential liquidity constraints,
asset-value declines, and weakening borrower repayment capacity as areas of
ongoing supervisory attention.
