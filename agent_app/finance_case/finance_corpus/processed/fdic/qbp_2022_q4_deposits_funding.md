# FDIC Q4 2022 Quarterly Banking Profile
## Deposits and Funding Conditions

Total deposits declined for a third consecutive quarter.

Total industry deposits were approximately $19.2 trillion in the fourth
quarter of 2022, down 0.7 percent from the third quarter.

The decline was driven by uninsured deposits, while insured deposits increased.

Competitive pressures to raise deposit rates may increase as market interest
rates remain elevated.

Deposit costs had been rising more slowly than loan yields, although this gap
may narrow as market rates stabilize or decline.
